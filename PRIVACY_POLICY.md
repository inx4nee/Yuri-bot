# Privacy Policy for Yuri (Discord Bot)

**Effective Date:** March 20, 2026  
**Last Updated:** May 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Yuri ("the Bot") collects, uses, stores, and protects your data when you interact with it on Discord. By using the Bot or adding it to your server, you consent to the practices described below.

---

## 1. What Data We Collect

To function properly as a conversational AI and social bot, Yuri collects the following data:

* **Discord Account Data:** User IDs, Display Names, Avatar URLs, Account Creation Dates, and Server Roles.
* **Presence Data:** Current status (Online, DND) and rich presence activities (e.g., listening to Spotify, playing games) — captured only at the exact moment a command is run, not monitored continuously.
* **Message Content:** Text, uploaded images, and audio attachments (voice notes) are processed **only** when the Bot is explicitly mentioned (`@Yuri`) or directly replied to.
* **Command Usage:** Data generated by specific commands, such as target User IDs for `/crush`, `/grudge`, or bug reports submitted via `/feedback`.
* **Server Data:** Guild/Server IDs and specific channel IDs configured by admins for features like `/confess`.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly to provide the Bot's core features:

* **AI Inference:** Sending your text, images, or audio to our AI providers to generate Yuri's responses.
* **Conversation Memory:** Maintaining a short-term history of your chat with the Bot so it can remember the context of the conversation.
* **Social Commands:** Analyzing your Discord profile and chat history to power personalized features like `/roast`, `/rate`, and `/ship`.

**We do not use your data to train our own machine learning models, nor do we sell your data.**

---

## 3. Data Storage and Retention

| Data Type | Retention |
|---|---|
| Conversation history | **30 days** — automatically purged via a MongoDB TTL index |
| Server configuration | Until manually changed by a server admin |
| Crush and grudge records | Until the match resolves or an admin removes the record |
| Feedback submissions | Until reviewed and manually deleted by the developer |

All data is stored in a MongoDB database encrypted at rest. All connections are encrypted in transit.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

Because Yuri is an AI bot, your inputs (messages, images, audio) are transmitted to third-party APIs for processing. All transmissions occur over HTTPS.

| Provider | Data Sent | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Google (Gemini API)** | Message text, images | Primary AI text and vision generation |
| **Groq** | Message text, images, audio | Fallback AI generation; voice note transcription via Whisper |
| **Together AI** | Message text | Optional fine-tuned model fallback — only contacted when all primary providers are unavailable. If the operator has not configured this integration, your data is **never** sent to Together AI. |
| **DuckDuckGo** | Search query derived from your message | Web search for up-to-date context on information-seeking queries |

---

## 5. Data Deletion and Your Rights

You have complete control over your conversational data.

* **Instant Self-Deletion:** Use `/clearhistory` in any server where the Bot is present to instantly and permanently delete your entire conversation history. This action is irreversible.
* **Manual Requests:** To request deletion of other data (feedback submissions, grudge records, matchmaking data), use the `/feedback` command. The developer will confirm deletion once processed.
* **Automatic Expiry:** Conversation history is deleted automatically after 30 days with no action required on your part.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not directed at users under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has submitted data to the Bot, please contact the developer via `/feedback`.

---

## 7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, the **Last Updated** date at the top of this document will be revised. Continued use of the Bot after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

---

## 8. Contact

For privacy questions, data deletion requests, or bug reports, use the **`/feedback`** command in any server where the Bot is present. Your message and User ID are routed directly to the developer's private inbox.
